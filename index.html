MEET JAY HARPER: CREATING HEARTFELT QUEER ROMANCE WITHOUT VENDOR BOUNDARIES

By Special Correspondent — Published for Readers Everywhere

Welcome to my cozy corner of the internet. I am Jay Harper, a gay romance author dedicated to writing stories about love, healing, second chances, and the beautiful messiness of finding home in another person.

Having published stories across traditional avenues, I envisioned this website as a pure "author sandbox"—a place where readers can browse my complete portfolio and read full-length stories for free using an embedded browser e-reader, completely free from vendor lock-in or subscription algorithms.

"Romance isn't just about the ending—it's about every heartbeat, pause, and shared whisper that builds the bridge between two souls."

When I'm not writing, you can find me sipping chamomile tea, tending to my vintage typewriter collection, or walking along rainy shorelines thinking up dialogue for characters who refuse to go to sleep.

Jay Harper

Gay Romance Author & Press Founder

Writing Dispatch October 12, 2026

Why I Build Free E-Readers Instead of Vendor Paywalls

As an indie romance author, nothing brings me greater joy than placing my stories directly into the hands of readers. By embedding a web-based e-reader into my site, anyone can read my works on their phone, tablet, or laptop without needing proprietary apps or subscriptions.

"Stories are bridges. The shorter the distance between the page and the reader's heart, the better."
