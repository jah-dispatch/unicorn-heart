Short Story 24,000 Words

Sunflower Lane

Coffee's just liquid hot anxiety—and Calvin Collins sells corporate confidence in a bottle!

As a high-flying pharma rep, Calvin hit rock bottom when he snapped at a tired barista. But that barista was none other than Brody Decker running his family's cafe on four hours of sleep. When the enemies end up seated together on a flight to Seattle, sparks fly into a fiery, impulsive hookup.

They part ways with no numbers exchanged—until Brody looks out his bedroom window on Sunflower Lane and finds his mystery hookup moving in next door.

But, when a ruthless corporate chain threatens the family cafe, Calvin uses his shark-like tactics to help. Now, they're sneaking through bedroom windows, dodging Brody's overly-involved parents, and hiding a secret romance. Can a corporate burnout and a small-town barista roast up a happy ending, or will their romance burn out before the morning rush?

Short Story 25,000 Words

Aspen Landing

Greg Thorn prefers winter solitude. Tucked away in his San Juan cabin in a red-and-black plaid suit and sparkly top hat, with only his cat Chestnut for company, Greg is Aspen Landing’s proudest, most eccentric Scrooge-lite.

Enter Caleb: a hopelessly romantic, holiday-obsessed travel writer stranded by a brutal Colorado blizzard right on Greg’s doorstep. Caleb is hunting for authentic Christmas magic; Greg is just trying to protect his quiet life. But as they’re snowed in together sharing hot cocoa, Caleb’s infectious joy begins to melt Greg’s defenses.

Between fiery banter and crackling fireplaces, Greg realizes this bright-eyed optimist might just be the ultimate holiday surprise. Can a grumpy older recluse and a younger romantic find a spark that lasts past December? Aspen Landing is a cozy, low-angst, age-gap holiday romance about finding unexpected warmth in the frostiest season.

MEET JAY HARPER: CREATING HEARTFELT QUEER ROMANCE WITHOUT VENDOR BOUNDARIES

By Special Correspondent — Published for Readers Everywhere

Welcome to my cozy corner of the internet. I’m Jay Harper, an MM romance author dedicated to writing stories grounded in deep emotion, genuine connection, and the beautiful messiness of finding home in another person.

If the name sounds familiar, you might remember my work under Jack Harper. I’ve stepped into a fresh era as Jay to bring a whole new flavor of gay romance to the table—stories with richer depth, bigger heart, and a fresh perspective on love.

"I've committed to writing stories free of charge, for anyone's budget to allow readers to enjoy my work without barriers. My stories are for everyone, and I want them to be accessible to all."

When I’m not writing, you can usually find me surrounded by music, obsessing over cheese (in all its magnificent forms), or indulging in my ultimate comfort meal: a rare steak with extra mushrooms and fluffy mashed potatoes at Texas Roadhouse.

I keep my creative spark alive with constant playlists—and while Taylor Swift used to hold a major spot in my rotation, she’s currently just 'okay' in my book. Keeping me company, keeping me grounded, and strictly keeping me on my toes through every chapter is Ivy, my wonderfully fluffy feline companion.

Writing Dispatch October 14, 2026

A Quick Note on AI & Workflow

Just a quick lowdown on my stance where AI is concerned. I don’t use it to influence my personal writing and only as a creativity enhancer. I use it to edit and advance my craft. I use it to do fun graphic projects from time to time just in the name of fun. When you have the willingness to walk a day in my schizophrenic shoes, you’ll learn quickly just how much this technology benefits me with my daily struggles.

"Peace out - Jay"
Character Beats October 18, 2026

Story Notes

In Sunflower Lane, I'm writing my most highly wired and complexly sunshiny characters yet. Calvin is something out of a ceareal box, flying on sugar and caffeine, and well, the pedaling of pharmaceuticals. This story is fun to write, but I'm taking my time just to make sure that I don't overwelm myself with too much. As you know, I suffer from mental health struggles. So, writing for me these days are only supposed to be nothing but fun for me. I am, however, thinking this story will be ready by November 13, 2026.
